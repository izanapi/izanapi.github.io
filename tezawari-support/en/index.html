Privacy Policy 日本語

Play an instrument

Tap an instrument name at the top of the screen to switch. Use KEY and SCALE to tune it, and VOICE to choose its sound.

HANABI
Swipe across a circle of strings. Move inward to add harmonics.
LOOM
Play the vertical strings for a melody. Brush the horizontal strings for resonance or a low drone.
KALEIDO
Touch different parts of the kaleidoscope to change pitch. Hold to rotate it slowly or slide sideways to change the mirror count.

Record a two-bar loop

Tap REC and start playing. The first note starts a two-bar recording that plays back automatically. Tap PAUSE to stop it, PLAY to resume, or CLEAR to erase it.

When you switch away and return to an instrument, its recorded loop is paused. Quitting the app clears the loops.

If you can’t hear anything

If the sound button shows MUTED, tap it to return to SOUND. Check the VOLUME slider, your device’s volume, and whether sound is routed to headphones or another output.
