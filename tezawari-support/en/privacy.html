Three Instruments Privacy Policy

Support 日本語

This policy applies to Three Instruments for iPhone and iPad, provided by izanapi.

App data

Playing and recording two-bar loops are processed on your device. The app records your playing within Three Instruments and does not access the microphone.

The app does not collect or transmit personal data, performance data, or usage data. It does not use advertising, tracking, or cloud sync.

Retention and deletion

Loops and instrument settings are kept in memory while the app is running. You can remove a loop with CLEAR. Quitting the app clears its loops and settings.

Contact

Information received in a support email is used to respond and look into the issue. For questions about privacy or requests to delete information you provided in an email, contact gotrio5@gmail.com.
